                            Solving a linear system

We want x such that Ax = b, where

  A = ⎡ 2  1 ⎤,  b = ⎡ 3 ⎤
      ⎣ 1  3 ⎦       ⎣ 5 ⎦

 1 Compute the determinant: det A = 2 · 3 − 1 · 1 = 5.
 2 Invert:
            1
     A⁻¹ = ─── ⎡  3  −1 ⎤
            5  ⎣ −1   2 ⎦

╭───────┬────────╮
│ Step  │ Result │
├───────┼────────┤
│ det A │ 5      │
│ x₁    │ 4/5    │
╰───────┴────────╯
